# Customer Performance Analysis

### Understanding Customer Behavior & Revenue Contribution Through Data Analytics

A data analysis project focused on understanding customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and revenue contribution to support better business decisions.

</div>

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data for more than **18,000 customers** to understand customer characteristics and identify the segments that generate the highest revenue.

The main objective is to move beyond assumptions and use data-driven insights to understand which customer segments contribute the most to business performance.

Using data analysis and visualization, this project explores customer demographics, family status, gender distribution, and revenue contribution across different segments.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify the most valuable customer age segments.
- Analyze revenue contribution across customer demographics.
- Understand the relationship between having children and customer revenue.
- Compare revenue contribution by gender.
- Identify customer segments that represent opportunities for business growth.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several interesting findings:

### 1. Revenue by Age Group

- Customers aged **31–40** generated the highest revenue.
- Their total revenue reached approximately **79 million**.
- This result challenged the initial assumption that younger customers would generate the highest revenue.

### 2. Customer Family Status

- Approximately **71% of customers have children**.
- Customers with children represent the largest segment of the customer base.
- Customers with children in the United States generated more than **77 million** in revenue.

### 3. Revenue by Gender

Revenue contribution was almost equally distributed between male and female customers.

| Gender | Revenue | Contribution |
|:---|---:|---:|
| Female | 154M | 50.3% |
| Male | 153M | 49.7% |

These findings indicate that revenue is not concentrated in one gender segment.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can consider the following actions:

- Focus on customers aged 31–40 when developing targeted marketing campaigns.
- Develop marketing strategies that address the needs of customers with children.
- Maintain balanced marketing strategies across male and female customers.
- Use customer segmentation to identify high-value customer groups.
- Base marketing and business decisions on actual customer behavior rather than assumptions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data analysis and data processing.
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX:** Business calculations and performance metrics.
